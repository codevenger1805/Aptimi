## 🚀 APTIMI is Live

From **“I want to become a Product Manager”** to **“okay, what am I actually supposed to do today?”** — APTIMI is built to bridge that gap.

It turns career goals into a structured journey with **skill assessments, personalized roadmaps, focused work sessions, internship tracking, career readiness, notes, and actionable next steps** — all in one place.

After a lot of planning, designing, debugging, and probably an unnecessary number of browser tabs… it’s finally out in the wild. 🌱

### ✨ Try it yourself

👉 **[Open the Live APTIMI App](https://aptimi-platform.netlify.app)**

Go set a career goal, explore the roadmap, break something, find something useful, or just click around.  
**APTIMI is officially live — your career plan can stop living in 17 different tabs now.** 🫡

> Built to turn career goals into action — one small step at a time.
